# Tyyli Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 14, 2026**

Tyyli is a personal styling app that helps you organize clothing and generate outfit suggestions. This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

## Information stored on your device

Tyyli stores your closet photos, clothing details, style profile, saved schedules, outfit picks, and outfit history locally on your device. Tyyli does not require an account. You can delete individual closet items and history entries in the app, reset all app data from Profile, or remove the app to remove its local data.

## Information used by AI features

When you choose an AI feature, Tyyli sends only the information needed to complete that request to the Tyyli server. Depending on the feature, this may include:

- a clothing photo for clothing analysis or visual product search;
- clothing names and metadata, style preferences, weather, saved plans, and recent outfit history for outfit recommendations;
- your question and relevant closet or profile context for the AI stylist; or
- selected onboarding answers for a generated style identity.

The Tyyli server uses Anthropic's Claude service for AI responses and SerpApi for visual product search. These services process the request to return results to the app. Tyyli does not use this information for advertising and does not sell personal information.

## Weather and location

If you allow location access, the app uses your approximate coordinates to request current weather and a city label from Open-Meteo and OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service. Location is used to show local weather and improve outfit suggestions. Tyyli does not intentionally store a history of your location on its server.

## App security and usage limits

On supported Apple devices, Tyyli uses Apple's App Attest to confirm that paid AI requests come from a genuine copy of the app. Short-lived verification challenges and hashed app-installation identifiers are stored in Upstash Redis to prevent replay and enforce usage limits. Tyyli does not use these identifiers for advertising or cross-app tracking.

## Data retention

Closet data and profile data remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. The Tyyli server processes AI inputs to answer the request and is not designed as cloud storage for your closet or profile. Short-lived security challenges expire automatically; limited usage counters may be retained long enough to enforce the configured request window. Third-party processors may retain data according to their own policies and legal obligations.

## Children

Tyyli is not directed to children under 13.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top will show the latest revision.
